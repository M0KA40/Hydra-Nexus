# Hydra Nexus 4.0

This update introduces **Nexus Discovery**: a new look that preserves the foundations of the original Hydra experience while extensively redesigning its pages and details to make them more beautiful and interactive.

## Nexus Discovery interface
- **Home:** a cinematic redesign with a slider featuring four of the most active games by concurrent players on Steam. Interactive sections connected to Steam help you discover games and follow new releases in one place. Improved imagery and navigation make Home a more inspiring place to find your next game.
- **Catalogue:** improved game arrangement and presentation for clearer browsing that fits the new interface.
- **Library:** refreshed visuals and a subtle glass effect when hovering over game covers.
- **Game page:** reorganized into separate sections for easier access to details. A new Updates section uses developer news from Steam for announcements and patch notes; further improvements are planned.
- **Downloads:** redesigned to present the downloading game and its progress more clearly and vividly.

## Play Activity and game statistics
- Redesigned Play Activity with a richer, more interactive presentation of your play history.
- Improved views of playtime, games, active days and sessions, with navigation between periods.
- Updated per-game statistics with more detail about days, sessions and playtime, and smoother transitions that fit Discovery.
- Redesigned weekly and monthly recap notifications with game images from the period and colors suited to your selected theme.

## Local and cloud backups
- Local backups return: use local storage, cloud storage or both.
- Improved backup management and status presentation.
- File and destination review, integrity verification before restoration, explicit replacement confirmation and safety copies of affected files remain in place.

## Library Release Calendar
- Optional Release Calendar plugin, enabled from Nexus Plugins, with direct access from your library.
- Upcoming and past releases appear on the days of the month; games sharing a release date are grouped together.
- Game covers appear inside the calendar, and you can open each game's page directly.
- Improved release-date reading and refresh, with saved data used when fresh data cannot be retrieved.
- Dates that are not final, such as a year only or TBA, stay in a separate list without an invented release day.

## Achievement notifications
- New Nexus Discovery design and motion, with normal, hidden, rare and platinum variants.
- Try notification in Settings previews the design without unlocking achievements or changing their history.
- Choose between Nexus and Hydra's original design. Customize the sound, with Nexus's default sound kept separate from Hydra's original sound.
- More responsive preview controls with clear waiting and error states. More notification designs and sound options are planned.

## Nexus 4.0 installer
- Setup and update launcher refreshed with Nexus colors, improved typography, buttons and icon.
- Automatic Hydra path detection and manual selection when detection is unavailable.
- Install Nexus and Remove Nexus, including updates and repair of an existing installation.
- Improved Hydra shutdown before the operation and restart after success; the installer then closes automatically.

This is a new beginning for Nexus Discovery, with more development and refinement ahead. I have spent a long time working on its details, and I hope you enjoy exploring it as much as I enjoyed building it.

Share your experiences and suggestions. You can add me as a friend on Hydra using the button on the Nexus page in Settings. Thank you to everyone whose feedback and ideas helped improve the project.

**Together, let's make Hydra the perfect place for gamers.**
