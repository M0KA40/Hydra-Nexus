# Hydra Nexus 4.2 — Release notes

New features give you more control, distinctive achievement sounds and a more polished experience throughout Hydra Nexus.

![Hydra Nexus 4.2](../../../assets/promo/release-4.2.png)

## Game Pause

Take a break and pick up exactly where you left off. Pause supported single-player games, including cutscenes, even when the game has no pause option. Press **F5** to pause and press it again to resume, even from another window. You can also customize the shortcut to suit you.

**For supported single-player games only. Do not use in online games or games with anti-cheat.**

## SteamGridDB Artwork

More ways to make your library your own. Connect your **SteamGridDB** account’s API key to recover missing artwork results in Hydra’s **Customization**, including **Icon, Logo, Library Hero and Cover**. Choose from the artwork available on SteamGridDB and give each game the look you want.

## Nexus Dynamic — Achievement sounds

A distinct sound for every achievement. Nexus Dynamic provides dedicated sounds for **Normal, Hidden, Rare and Platinum** achievements, matching the moment and the type of achievement you unlock. Try them in the achievement notification settings. The previous sound remains available as **Nexus Legacy**.

## Custom sound management

Add a collection of your favorite sounds, choose the one you want to use and remove those you no longer need from a clearer, easier-to-use list.

## Developer blogs — Updates

Follow news and updates from each game’s developers in a clearer, better-organized format. Posts now support the languages published by the developer and supported by Hydra Nexus. Your interface language is used when available, and you can switch between the languages available for each post.

## Feedback — Get in touch

A dedicated section lets you send messages directly to the Hydra Nexus developer. Share your suggestions, ideas for future features and feedback about the tool.

## Translation improvements

Improved translations across supported languages, with clearer, more consistent wording throughout the interface.

## A lighter installer and general improvements

Installation files have been reorganized, cleaned up and compressed, cutting the installer size roughly in half, alongside interface improvements and a range of bug fixes.
